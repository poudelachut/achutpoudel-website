ABOUT ME

A Multidisciplinary
Professional Journey

A multidisciplinary professional journey shaped by diverse responsibilities, challenging environments, and continuous growth.

With 20+ years of professional experience, the journey has evolved through diverse roles across human resources, banking, business operations, administration, law, compliance, branding, and organizational development.

Each chapter has brought different responsibilities and challenges, building a practical understanding of how people, organizations, business decisions, and systems work together.

The professional foundation combines MBA in Human Resource Management, MBS in Finance, and LLB, bringing together perspectives from management, finance, and law.

Professional qualification as an Advocate further strengthens the ability to understand organizational matters through both business and legal perspectives.

The approach to leadership is grounded in integrity, accountability, continuous learning, and practical action—with a strong belief that meaningful progress comes from listening carefully, thinking strategically, and turning ideas into results.
