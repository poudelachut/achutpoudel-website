ABOUT ME

A Career Shaped by Experience, Learning & Perspective.

With more than 20 years of professional experience, the career journey has evolved across human resources, banking, administration, business operations, law, compliance, branding, and organizational development.

Working across different industries and organizational environments has provided exposure to both people-focused challenges and business-critical decisions. This experience has developed a practical understanding of how people, processes, strategy, and organizational priorities connect.

The academic foundation combines MBA in Human Resource Management, MBS in Finance, and LLB, bringing together perspectives from management, finance, and law. Professional qualification as an Advocate adds another dimension to understanding governance, compliance, contracts, and organizational matters.

Over the years, leadership has increasingly become less about position and more about responsibility — creating clarity, developing people, managing change, solving problems, and helping organizations move forward with confidence.

The professional approach is grounded in integrity, accountability, continuous learning, collaboration, and practical execution.
